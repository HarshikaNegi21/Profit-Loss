## Sales Report

### Objectives

- Create a [Customer Performance Report](https://github.com/HarshikaNegi21/Profit-Loss/blob/HarshikaNegi21-patch-1/CustomerPerformanceReport.pdf).  
- Conduct a comparative analysis between market performance and sales targets in the [Market vs. Targets Report](https://github.com/HarshikaNegi21/Profit-Loss/blob/HarshikaNegi21-patch-1/MarketPerformanceVsTarget.pdf).  

### Purpose

Empower businesses to monitor and evaluate their sales activities and overall performance.

### Importance

Identify sales patterns and track key performance indicators (KPIs) to inform strategic decisions.

### Role of Reports

- Determine optimal customer discount schemes.  
- Facilitate data-driven negotiations with clients.  
- Highlight expansion opportunities in high-potential regions.

## Finance Report

### Objectives

- Generate Profit & Loss (P&L) statements by [Fiscal Year](https://github.com/HarshikaNegi21/Profit-Loss/blob/HarshikaNegi21-patch-1/P%26LReportbyFiscalYear.pdf) and by [Month](https://github.com/HarshikaNegi21/Profit-Loss/blob/HarshikaNegi21-patch-1/P%26LReportbyMonth.pdf).  
- Produce P&L statements segmented by [Market](https://github.com/HarshikaNegi21/Profit-Loss/blob/HarshikaNegi21-patch-1/P%26LReportbyMarket.pdf).  

### Purpose

Evaluate financial performance to support executive decision-making and stakeholder communications.

### Importance

Benchmark results against industry standards and historical periods, forming the basis for budgeting and forecasting.

### Role of Reports

- Align financial planning with organizational strategy.  
- Instill confidence in investors and internal stakeholders regarding financial health.
